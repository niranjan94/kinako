# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy covers the Kinako desktop app, command-line server, and iOS app ("Kinako"), published by Niranjan Rajendran.

## Summary

Kinako does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking by default. The mobile app talks only to Kinako servers that you run and pair with it.

## Data the app handles

- **Server connections.** When you pair the mobile app with a Kinako server, the app stores that server's address and access credentials on your device, in the iOS Keychain where applicable. They are sent only to that server.
- **Your work.** Projects, threads, prompts, agent output, and file changes live on the Kinako server you run. The mobile app downloads them from your server to display them and may cache them on your device. They are not sent to us.
- **Coding agents.** Your server runs coding agents such as Claude Code or Codex using your own provider accounts. What those providers receive is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.
- **Voice input.** If you use voice input, the recording is transcribed by the Kinako server you are connected to and is not sent to us.
- **Camera.** The camera is used only to scan pairing QR codes. Images are not stored or transmitted.
- **Photos.** The app can save images to your photo library when you ask it to. It does not read your library.
- **Local network.** The app uses local network access only to reach Kinako servers on your network or tailnet.

## Data sent to us

None. Kinako builds do not send product usage data or crash reports to the publisher. If you run a Kinako server and choose to configure your own telemetry or diagnostics endpoint, that data goes to the destination you configured.

## Third parties

Apple may provide crash reports and usage information for TestFlight and App Store builds if you have opted in to sharing them with app developers in your device settings. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Children

Kinako is a developer tool and is not directed at children.
